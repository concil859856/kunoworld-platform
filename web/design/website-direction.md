# KunoWorld — Think it. Make it move.

A full creative platform website, built around cinematic images and actual generated films. The light editorial foundation now showcases nine genuinely different subjects: night cinema, product design, fashion, science fiction, an immense library, paper animation, wildlife fantasy, glass, and kinetic art. The studio remains a separate working surface.

## Page map

- `/`: full-screen selectable films, creative workflows, image stories, lantern-library interlude, curated screening room, privacy, developer introduction, journal, and calls to create.
- `/studio`: connected encrypted generation workspace with model-aware controls and scene deep links.
- `/showcase`: nine-film screening room with genre filters, full video playback, prompt descriptions, and studio links.
- `/use-cases`: advertising/product, cinematic worlds, vertical editorial, and reference-to-motion workflows.
- `/models`: actual KunoWorld model capabilities and estimated profile costs.
- `/developers`: JavaScript/Python SDK examples and request journey.
- `/docs`: setup, two-terminal development quickstart, creation modes, receipts, troubleshooting, project status.
- `/api`: gateway endpoints, request/response descriptions, authentication, and error shapes.
- `/blog`: five complete articles covering directing, camera movement, provenance, product films, and vertical composition.
- `/about`, `/privacy`: vision, protocol boundaries, and explicit development status.

## Motion and media

Manual hero film selection; no automatic carousel. Native video loops with visible pause controls, near-viewport loading, offscreen pause, and reduced-motion handling. Scroll-revealed editorial blocks, a scroll-responsive type strip, immersive parallax, and existing studio depth effects. Mobile uses native scrolling and single-column reading layouts.

All nine visible still/video pairs are original, generated through OpenRouter with the user's authorized key. They are creative studies, not KunoWorld GPU benchmarks. The five latest subjects replace every ocean image and film across the landing page, model covers, journal, About, developer banner, and studio. The new landing film cards differ from the hero selection. Gallery categories follow the actual subjects, including Animation and Art & motion.
